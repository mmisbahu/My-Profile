Education & Language in Canada

Free classes, school enrollment, and improving your English or French

FREE English & French Classes

No cost, funded by government! These programs are FREE for newcomers to improve your language skills.
LINC Program (Language Instruction for Newcomers)

Official government program offering FREE English classes

  • Who: Permanent residents and refugees
  • Cost: FREE
  • Levels: Beginner to Advanced
  • Where: Community centers, schools, libraries
  • Schedule: Daytime, evening, weekend classes

How to find LINC: Contact your settlement agency or visit canada.ca

Enrolling Children in School

Steps to Enroll:
1
Find your school boundary

Check which school covers your residential area

2
Gather required documents

Proof of residency, birth certificate, proof of vaccination

3
Visit the school office

Fill out enrollment forms and register your child

4
Start school

Get schedule and supplies list. Attend orientation if available.

School is FREE! Public schools in Canada don't charge tuition for children of permanent residents (K-Grade 12).

French-Language Education (Quebec)

French is official language in Quebec. Most schools teach IN FRENCH except for anglophone schools (if eligible).
Language of Instruction Rules:
  • Francophone schools: Instruction in French (most schools)
  • English schools: Only eligible if parent(s) were educated in English in Canada
  • Before arrival: Apply to English board if eligible

Learn French: Your child will learn French through school. Adults can also take free/affordable French courses.

Post-Secondary Education (College/University)

University

Tuition (International): $20,000-35,000/year

Domestic (PR): $5,000-15,000/year (varies)

Duration: 3-4 years for bachelor's

College

Tuition: $3,000-10,000/year

Programs: 2-3 year diplomas (practical skills)

Great for: Trades, tech, business

As a PR: You can get student loans and bursaries. Many provinces also have free/subsidized tuition programs.

Skills Training & Trades

Vocational Training Programs (Trades):

If you want to learn a trade (plumber, electrician, carpenter, etc.):

  • Community colleges - 2 year programs
  • Apprenticeships - On-the-job training
  • Cost: $3,000-10,000
  • Jobs: High demand, good pay ($60,000+)
