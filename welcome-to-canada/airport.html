Airport Arrival

Your complete guide to landing in Canada and getting through CBSA

Before You Land

Documents You MUST Have
  • Passport - Valid travel document
  • COPR (Confirmation of Permanent Residence) - This is critical!
  • PR Letter - From IRCC confirming your permanent residence status
  • Medical Exam Results - If required
  • Police Clearance - If required

CBSA Checkpoint (Border Control)

What Will They Ask?

  • Purpose of your visit to Canada
  • Your permanent residence status
  • Where you studied/worked
  • Where you plan to live
  • If you have family in Canada

Pro Tips

  • Speak clearly and don't rush
  • Be honest and direct in your answers
  • Keep documents organized and easy to access
  • Don't volunteer extra information
  • Be polite and respectful

What to Declare

Prohibited Items

  • Weapons, firearms, explosives
  • Illegal drugs
  • Certain plants and seeds
  • Protected animal products
  • Counterfeit goods

Allowed Items

  • Personal clothing and toiletries
  • Electronics (laptop, phone)
  • Prescription medications
  • Food (declared properly)
  • Gifts (limited value)

Currency & Money

If you're bringing $10,000 CAD or more (or equivalent in any currency), you MUST declare it to CBSA.

Useful Info:

  • Canada's currency is the Canadian Dollar (CAD)
  • ATMs are widely available at the airport and city centers
  • Credit/debit cards are accepted everywhere
  • Consider opening a bank account quickly (see Banking section)

Canada Line (SkyTrain)

Airport bus/shuttle

Multiple services available

Taxi / Rideshare

Available at the terminal

Cost: $25-45 | Time: 15-30 mins

Taxi / Uber

Direct to your location

Cost: $30-50+ | Time: 30-45 mins
