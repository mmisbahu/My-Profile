Settlement Agencies

Settlement agencies are FREE! They provide support, orientation, job training, language classes, and more - all at no cost.

Immigration Lawyers & Consultants

Be careful! Only use licensed lawyers (member of Law Society) or certified immigration consultants (member of ICCRC). Scammers exist!
How to Find Legitimate Help:
Law Society of Ontario - Find Lawyer
Canada Legal Services Directory
ICCRC - Find Certified Consultant
Cost:
  • Initial consultation: $200-500
  • Full immigration case: $1,000-5,000+
  • Some offer payment plans
