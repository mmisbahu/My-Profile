Government Documents & IDs

Everything you need to know about essential Canadian ID and documents

Social Insurance Number (SIN)

What is it?

A 9-digit number used for taxes, benefits, and employment in Canada.

Priority: HIGH - Apply ASAP

You need this before you can work!

How to Apply:
  • Visit Service Canada office - Find your nearest office at servicecanada.gc.ca
  • Bring required documents:
    • Passport or COPR
    • Proof of address (utility bill, rental contract)
    • Completed application form
  • Processing time: Same day or within a week
  • Cost: FREE
Online Application: You can now apply online through Service Canada's online services. Visit servicecanada.gc.ca

Permanent Residence (PR) Card

What is it?

Official proof of your permanent resident status. Required for travel.

Priority: MEDIUM - Apply within 1 month

Apply before traveling internationally

How to Apply:
  • Confirm your PR status: Log into IRCC My Account at ircc.canada.ca
  • Send completed form IMM 5444: Available at IRCC website
  • Include:
    • Copy of COPR
    • Valid passport
    • 2 passport photos
  • Mail to: IRCC PR Card office (address on form)
  • Processing time: 4-6 weeks
  • Cost: FREE
Travel Without PR Card: You can travel on your COPR + passport until PR card arrives.

Provincial Health Card

What is it?

Proof of health insurance coverage in your province. Required for medical services.

Priority: MEDIUM

Apply within 3 months of arrival

By Province:

Waiting period: 3 months

Apply at: ServiceOntario centers or online at ontario.ca

Documents needed: Proof of residency, passport, COPR

Cost: FREE

Waiting period: 3 months (can get temporary coverage)

Apply at: gov.bc.ca

Documents needed: Proof of residency, passport

Cost: FREE for most people

Waiting period: 3 months

Apply at: Service Alberta offices or alberta.ca

Documents needed: Proof of residency, ID

Cost: FREE

Waiting period: 3 months

Apply at: RAMQ offices or ramq.gouv.qc.ca

Documents needed: Proof of residency, passport

Cost: FREE

Driver's License

What is it?

Proof of driving eligibility. Can also be used as ID. Each province has different rules.

Priority: LOW - If you need to drive

General Steps:
  • Get an International Driving Permit in your home country (valid 1 year)
  • Take a written test on local traffic rules
  • Take a road/driving test
  • Bring documents: Passport, COPR, address proof, IDP
  • Cost: $50-300 depending on province
Temporary Replacement: Most provinces accept an International Driving Permit for 1 year. Get one before coming to Canada!

Canada Revenue Agency (CRA) Account

What is it?

Your online account for taxes, benefits, and government payments.

Priority: MEDIUM

Set up within 3-6 months

How to Set Up:
  • Online: Visit CRA My Account
  • By phone: 1-800-959-8722
  • In person: Visit a CRA office
  • You'll need: SIN, passport, proof of address
  • Cost: FREE
File Your Taxes: Even if you had no income, file a tax return to establish tax residency and claim benefits like GST Credit.
