Understanding Rental Costs

Average Rent by City (1-Bedroom)
  • Toronto: $2,000-2,500/month
  • Vancouver: $2,200-2,800/month
  • Calgary: $1,300-1,700/month
  • Montreal: $1,200-1,600/month

Prices vary by neighborhood, distance from downtown, and amenities.

What's Included in Rent?

  • Utilities: Heat, water, electricity - sometimes included, often separate
  • Parking: Extra $50-200/month if not included
  • Internet: Usually not included ($50-100/month)

Renting Without Canadian Credit

Most landlords understand! Many have experience with newcomers and immigrants without Canadian credit history.
What You Can Provide Instead:
  • Proof of Income - Job offer letter, employment contract, or letter from employer
  • Bank Reference - Letter from your bank showing your account and deposits
  • International Credit Report - Get from previous country if available
  • References from Friends/Colleagues - Character references
  • Higher Deposit - Offer first & last month's rent upfront
  • Guarantor - Canadian friend/family member with good credit
Avoid Scams: Do NOT send money before seeing the apartment in person. Verify the landlord is legitimate before paying anything.

Know Your Tenant Rights

  • Landlord must give 60 days notice for rent increase
  • Security deposit = 1 month's rent (landlord must return)
  • Rent increase can't exceed guideline amount yearly
  • Landlord can't evict without proper notice (usually 60 days)
