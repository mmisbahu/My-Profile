Quick Tips for Your First Days

Important Documents

Keep your passport, COPR (Confirmation of Permanent Residence), and PR letter safe and easily accessible.

Get a SIM Card

Buy a SIM card at the airport or in a mall. Major carriers: Rogers, Bell, Telus, Freedom Mobile.

Language Support

Free English & French classes available through LINC (Immigrant Settlement Services). Visit the "Education" section.

Know Your Rights

As a PR, you have the same rights as Canadian citizens. Contact a settlement agency for help.
